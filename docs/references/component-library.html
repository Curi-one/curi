The reader07
Lesson reader · the surface that matters most
Day 04 · Game theory · Beginner

Why two rational people talk themselves into a bad room

Everyone acting sensibly can still walk everyone into the wall. The trap is not stupidity, it is structure.

Two petrol stations sit opposite each other. Either can hold its price or cut it. If both hold, both do well. If both cut, both do badly. If one cuts alone, it takes the whole street.

Look at it from inside one station. Whatever the other does, cutting is the better move. So you cut. So do they. Both of you land in the outcome neither of you wanted, and neither of you made a mistake.

Takeaways
  • 01A dominant strategy is one that wins regardless of what the other side does.
  • 02Individually rational choices can produce a collectively worse result.
  • 03Repeating the game changes it. Reputation is what makes cooperation rational.

680px column, 16px body at 1.75 line height. Two Vermilion elements appear here, the pull-quote rule and the takeaway numerals, which is one more than the rule allows. Pick one per lesson: use the numerals when there is no pull quote, and drop the numerals to Silver when there is.

Using this library

  • Accent budgetBefore shipping a screen, count the Vermilion. One. The wordmark underline, or a CTA underline, or a pull-quote rule, or an accent card. Not two of them.
  • Never invent a variantFour button types, four card types. A need for a fifth is almost always a layout problem in disguise.
  • No semantic colourCorrect is Ink. Wrong is dimmed. Errors are Silver. Destructive is Mid. Green and red do not exist in this system.
  • Numbers are typographyStreaks, stats and progress counts are set in Fraunces at display scale and never boxed. Metadata numbers are Mono and small. Nothing sits in between.
  • Fractions, not percentages"7 of 14 lessons" tells a reader where they are in a story. "50%" tells them a statistic about themselves.
  • Zero radius, zero shadowIf a component has either, it came from somewhere else. This is the fastest tell that a screen has drifted.
